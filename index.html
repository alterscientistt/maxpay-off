Quick Guides

📈 How to Buy / Sell Stocks

1. Go to the Invest page.
2. Select a stock (e.g. Tesla, Apple).
3. Enter Amount and Click 'Invest'.
4. To Sell, go to 'Profile' and select 'Portfolio'.

💳 Using Paytm, PhonePe & Mobikwik

To Invest:
1. Select 'UPI' method in Invest.
2. Enter Amount (Min ₹100).
3. We will trigger a request to your Paytm, PhonePe, or Mobikwik app.
4. Approve payment in the app to credit funds instantly.

⚡ How to Get Free Recharges

You can use your Referral Bonus balance to pay for mobile recharges.
Go to UPI > Recharge, enter number, and select 'Pay from Bonus'.

FAQs & Tutorials

What is Max Pay?
Max Pay is an investment platform where you can grow your money through Instant (stock market) and Short Term investment plans with daily returns.
How do I start investing?
Deposit funds into your wallet, then go to the Instant or Short Term tab to choose a plan and invest.
What are Short Term plans?
Short Term plans let you invest for a fixed number of days. You earn a daily percentage reward credited to your balance every day.
How do I withdraw my money?
Go to the Portfolio tab and tap Withdraw. Enter your amount and UPI details. Withdrawals are processed by the admin.
How does the referral system work?
Share your referral ID with friends. When they sign up and invest, you earn a commission bonus credited to your wallet.
What is the Lucky Spin?
The Lucky Spin gives you a chance to win cash prizes and respins. You earn spins by making investments. Check the floating wheel button to spin!
How do I contact support?
Use the Contact Support button in Settings or tap the chatbot icon at the bottom-right to chat with our support team.
